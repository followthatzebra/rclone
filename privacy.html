Privacy Policy

Rclone AI Backups is a private-use configuration of the open-source Rclone application.

Purpose

The application enables its owner to transfer, back up and restore files using Google Drive.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive data only as authorised by the account owner. Files are transferred between the owner's devices and Google Drive.

Data Sharing

The application is not offered to third parties. It does not independently sell or share Google Drive data with advertisers.

Data Retention

Files remain under the account owner's control. Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Website

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not operate its own analytics or data collection forms.
